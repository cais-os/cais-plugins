# Cais Business Operations

Framework for business strategy, analysis, and communication across Cais
products.

## Pitch Structure

### Investor Pitch

1. **Problem** — What pain exists? (1 slide)
2. **Solution** — How do we solve it? (1 slide)
3. **Market** — How big is the opportunity? (1 slide)
4. **Product** — Demo or screenshots (1–2 slides)
5. **Traction** — Key metrics and growth (1 slide)
6. **Business model** — How we make money (1 slide)
7. **Team** — Why us? (1 slide)
8. **Ask** — What we need (1 slide)

### Product Brief

Use `/cais:brief` to generate a structured product brief:

- Problem statement
- Target audience
- Core features (MVP)
- Success metrics
- Competitive landscape

## Metrics Framework

### North Star Metrics

Each product should track:

- **Acquisition:** How users find the product
- **Activation:** First "aha moment"
- **Retention:** Do users come back?
- **Revenue:** How we make money
- **Referral:** Do users tell others?
