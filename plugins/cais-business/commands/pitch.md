---
description: Structure a pitch or proposal for a Cais product
argument-hint: [product-name]
---

Create a structured pitch for "$ARGUMENTS" using the Cais business-ops skill
framework.

Follow the investor pitch structure:

1. **Problem** — What pain exists?
2. **Solution** — How does $ARGUMENTS solve it?
3. **Market** — How big is the opportunity?
4. **Product** — Key features and differentiation
5. **Traction** — Metrics and growth (if available)
6. **Business Model** — How it makes money
7. **Team** — Why Cais is the right team
8. **Ask** — What's needed (funding, partnerships, etc.)

Check the product-context skill (from the cais-core plugin) for existing product
documentation. If the product doesn't have a context file yet, ask clarifying
questions to fill in the pitch sections.

Output the pitch as a structured document that can be adapted into slides or a
written proposal.
